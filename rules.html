SPECIAL CARDS

KNOW YOUR
CARDS!

SKIP

SKIP

Automatic success. The Challenge Card goes into your Bottom Deck and you draw 1.

+1

POWER SKIP

Automatic success plus the printed power value is added to your score.

+5

WILD

Create any Truth or Dare challenge you want. Worth +5.

↩

MIRROR

Reflect the challenge back. Mirrors can chain. Then draw 1.

↔

REVERSE

Switch the challenge from the selected neighbour to your other immediate neighbour.

The overall turn direction does NOT change.
WAIT... REVERSE?

IT DOESN'T
REVERSE THE TURN.

It switches WHO gets the challenge.

WHAT IF YOU FAIL?

THE CHALLENGE
KEEPS MOVING.

The same Challenge Card stays in play and passes clockwise.

If it returns to the original challenged player without being completed, that player takes it into their Bottom Deck.
